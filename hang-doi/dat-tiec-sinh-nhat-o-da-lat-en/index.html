Booking a birthday party in Da Lat without the hassle

Quick answer

A birthday in Da Lat runs smoothly when the restaurant already has three things: seating that keeps the group together, a grill at the table, and help with decoration. Tiệm Nướng & Chill Xóm Lèo (113 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 5 km from the centre) sets up birthdays and anniversaries free of charge, opens 15:00–23:00 every day including holidays, averages 200,000–250,000 VND per person, and has parking for about 15 cars. Book ahead on weekends via +84 76 452 7336.

What to sort out before booking

Birthdays in Da Lat usually go wrong over small things: booking a place with no long table so the group ends up split across two corners, or arriving to find the kitchen already closed. Settle these before you call.

  • Confirmed head count — this decides the table layout, not the number of people invited.
  • Time slot — if you want the sunset, you need to arrive earlier than a normal dinner.
  • Indoors or outdoors — Da Lat cools down fast after dark; groups with older relatives or small children should lean indoors.
  • Who handles decoration — ask directly whether the restaurant helps, so you don't carry a bag of balloons with nowhere to hang them.
  • How you're getting there — if you're driving, check for parking first.

Indoor or open-air seating?

This choice shapes the evening more than anything else, because Da Lat weather shifts through the day.

The open-air area gives you the view. At Xom Leo the outer row looks straight down into the Trai Mat valley, and late afternoon light falls across the flower gardens and greenhouses below. The trade-off: from around 19:00 the temperature drops quickly and mist can roll in.

Indoors is warmer and easier to talk in — better for a large group that wants to sing without the wind taking the sound away.

What many groups do: book outdoors from 17:00 to 19:00 for the sunset and photos, then move inside for the cake and the main meal. The menu and prices are identical in both areas — see the full menu.

Decoration: bring your own or let the venue handle it?

Bringing your own gives you control over the colours, but you need to arrive at least 30 minutes early to set up — and not every venue lets you fix things to walls or ceilings.

Xom Leo sets up birthdays, anniversaries and group parties free of charge — no decoration fee. Mention it when you book so the team can prepare, and say so if you want to keep it a surprise for the guest of honour.

A few things worth asking on the call, since every venue differs: whether you can bring a cake from outside, and whether there's somewhere for candles or your own music. One phone call beats improvising on arrival.

What does a birthday dinner cost?

At Xom Leo a full BBQ meal averages 200,000–250,000 VND per person. The 68-item menu runs from 12,000 to 320,000 VND, so a group can steer the total easily.

A worked example for six people, ordering enough to be full with something photogenic on the table:

  • 2 sharing slabs of grilled beef with cheese and salted egg — the usual centrepiece for a party table
  • 1 hotpot in the middle to keep things warm, such as chicken with la e herb at 300,000 VND
  • 2–3 individual grilled dishes, such as salt-and-pepper beef belly at 155,000 VND
  • Drinks and side dishes

That usually lands around 1.2–1.5 million VND for six. Full per-item pricing is on the menu page.

How far ahead should you book?

On a quiet weekday a small group can simply turn up. A birthday is different — you need tables together, and the kitchen needs time to prepare the setup.

  • Weekends and holidays: book ahead, and state your arrival time.
  • If you want the valley-facing outdoor row: book early, that row fills first.
  • Larger groups: give the closest head count you can so tables are joined correctly.

Book by phone on +84 76 452 7336, via Zalo, or by Facebook message. If your group is larger than the space left, the sister restaurant Tram Dung Chill is right next door at number 111.

The good time slots: sunset and the night train

If you want the evening to be memorable beyond the cake, aim for one of these two windows.

  • Before 17:30 — in time for the sun dropping over the valley, the best light of the day.
  • 18:40–19:00 and 20:45–21:10 — the two night trains leave Da Lat Station at 18:15 and 20:20 and take about 25 minutes, so they pass within sight of the restaurant in these windows.

The train only runs with enough passengers, so if you are building the evening around it, call Da Lat Station on 0263 3834 409 to confirm. Where to sit and how to time it is covered in our piece on the Da Lat BBQ with a train view.

What to order for a group

A simple rule for a party table: one sharing slab that everyone notices when it arrives, one hotpot to keep the table warm through the meal, and individual grilled dishes ordered as you go.

  • The sharing slab — Okachi beef with cheese and salted egg, 229,000 VND.
  • Something spicy — Thai-style spicy ribs, 259,000 VND.
  • Something different — salt-roasted sturgeon, 199,000 VND.
  • To keep warm — chicken and la e hotpot at 300,000 VND, or seafood hotpot at 320,000 VND.

When it gets cold the staff bring blankets and hot tea without being asked. Directions from the centre are on the directions page, and the space itself is covered on the experience page.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a charge for birthday decoration at Xom Leo?

No. The restaurant sets up birthdays, anniversaries and group parties free of charge. Just mention it when you book so the team can prepare.

What are the opening hours, and do you close on holidays?

Open 15:00–23:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays.

How far ahead should I book?

On weekdays a small group can turn up directly. For weekends, holidays, or if you want the valley-facing outdoor row, book ahead on +84 76 452 7336.

Is there car parking?

Yes, there is on-site parking for about 15 cars.

What time should I arrive for the sunset?

Arrive before 17:30. The stretch from about 15:00 to 17:30 is when the afternoon light falls across the Trai Mat valley.

How long is it from central Da Lat?

The restaurant is at 113 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, about 5 km from Xuan Huong Lake towards Trai Mat — 12–15 minutes by motorbike, 15–20 minutes by car.

How much does one person spend?

On average 200,000–250,000 VND per person for a full BBQ meal. The 68-item menu runs from 12,000 to 320,000 VND, so the total is easy to control.
