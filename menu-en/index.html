Da Lat BBQ Menu – Charcoal BBQ & Sturgeon Hotpot Xom Leo

Quick Summary

The Xom Leo Grill & Chill menu is organised into 13 sections: beef, pork, seafood, side dishes, hotpots and snacks, plus tea, soda, beer and spirits. Listed prices run from 12,000 VND (mineral water) up to 320,000 VND (sturgeon hotpot and seafood hotpot). The two best sellers are the salted-egg cheese beef slab at 209,000 VND and the sturgeon hotpot at 320,000 VND. We open 15:00–23:00 daily at 113 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward - Da Lat, Lam Dong.

What is on the Xom Leo menu and how much does it cost?

Xom Leo Grill & Chill (113 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward - Da Lat, Lam Dong) prints 68 items on its menu: 32 dishes and 36 drinks across 13 groups. Dishes run from 20,000đ (grilled German sausage) to 320,000đ (seafood hotpot, sturgeon hotpot). Drinks start at 12,000đ (bottled water) and reach 210,000đ (Export red wine 750ml). The beef, pork and seafood groups have 17 items priced 132,000đ – 259,000đ. Open 3:00pm – 11:00pm daily.

How much per person?

A full BBQ meal at Xom Leo usually comes to about 250,000đ – 300,000đ per person; ordering lighter or sharing in a bigger group costs less. Three budget sample orders, priced straight off the printed menu:

  • Two people, light meal: grilled beef belly 155,000đ, sweet potato croquettes 70,000đ, two peach orange lemongrass teas 88,000đ. Total 313,000đ — about 157,000đ per person.
  • Four people, sharing dishes: Thai spicy ribs 259,000đ, five-spice pork belly 132,000đ, shrimp spring rolls 95,000đ, highland fried rice 140,000đ, four Saigon Lagers 76,000đ. Total 702,000đ — about 176,000đ per person.
  • A group ordering hotpot: chicken hotpot with la e basil 300,000đ, mixed salad 95,000đ, four bottled waters 48,000đ. Total 443,000đ.

Anything that is not red meat? Seven seafood dishes at 150,000đ – 199,000đ, and four side dishes at 70,000đ – 95,000đ.
