Da Lat BBQ Menu – Charcoal BBQ & Sturgeon Hotpot Xom Leo

What is on the Xom Leo menu and how much does it cost?

Tiem Nuong & Chill Xom Leo (113 Huynh Tan Phat, Xuan Truong Ward, Da Lat) serves 68 items: 32 dishes and 36 drinks across 13 groups. Dishes run from 20,000đ (grilled German sausage) to 320,000đ (seafood hotpot, sturgeon hotpot). Drinks start at 12,000đ (bottled water) and reach 210,000đ (Export red wine 750ml). The grilled selection — beef, pork and seafood — has 17 items priced 132,000đ – 259,000đ. Open 3:00pm – 11:00pm daily.

Menu FAQ

What is the cheapest item? Bottled water at 12,000đ. The cheapest dish is grilled German sausage at 20,000đ.

What is the most expensive? Seafood hotpot and sturgeon hotpot, both 320,000đ.

Do you serve hotpot? Yes, three kinds: chicken with la e basil 300,000đ, seafood 320,000đ, sturgeon 320,000đ.

Anything that is not red meat? Seven seafood dishes at 150,000đ – 199,000đ, and four side dishes at 70,000đ – 95,000đ.
