Truc Lam Zen Monastery – A Serene Spiritual Retreat Nestled in the Mountains

Quick Summary

Truc Lam Zen Monastery overlooks Tuyen Lam Lake from Phung Hoang hill and is one of the calmest places around Da Lat. The guide covers its architecture, the cable car ride up and what to keep in mind when visiting.

Truc Lam Zen Monastery is one of the largest and most beautiful Zen monasteries in Vietnam, perched atop Phoenix Hill (Doi Phuong Hoang (Phoenix Hill)), approximately 5km south of downtown Da Lat. This sacred place offers a tranquil, meditative atmosphere surrounded by endless pine forests.

Architecture & Atmosphere

The monastery was built in traditional Vietnamese architectural style, featuring gracefully curved tile roofs and an imposing triple-gate entrance (Tam Quan). The expansive grounds are adorned with flower gardens, koi ponds, and rare plant species. From the main courtyard, visitors can gaze out over the breathtaking panorama of Tuyen Lam Lake stretching peacefully below.

Cable Car to the Monastery

Visitors can take the Robin Hill Cable Car to reach Truc Lam Zen Monastery while enjoying aerial views of Da Lat from above. Cable car tickets cost approximately 80,000 – 100,000 VND per person. The ride takes about 15 minutes and offers a stunning 360-degree view of the surrounding landscape.

Tips for Your Visit

Visitors should dress modestly and respectfully when entering the monastery. Please maintain silence and show respect for the monks and meditation spaces. A typical visit lasts between 1–2 hours.

Stop by Xom Leo After Your Visit

After exploring Truc Lam Zen Monastery, don't miss a visit to Xom Leo Grill & Chill to savor some irresistible grilled dishes. The restaurant stands out for its cozy atmosphere, open nature views, stunning sunsets, and the chance to watch the train pass by.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Truc Lam Zen Monastery? +

You should go in the early morning or late afternoon to enjoy the peaceful atmosphere and beautiful scenery at its best.

Should I take the cable car to the monastery? +

Yes, taking the cable car is a highly recommended experience as you can enjoy a panoramic view of Da Lat from above.

How long does a visit usually take? +

The visit usually takes about 1 to 2 hours depending on your itinerary.
