Top 30 breakfast restaurants in Da Lat that are loved by tourists

When the morning mist still lingers on the rooftops, Da Lat appears peaceful and poetic - it is also the ideal time to enjoy a proper breakfast in Da Lat. Starting a new day in Da Lat with attractive breakfast dishes, from familiar traditional dishes to unique variations, is an indispensable experience for any tourist.

Not simply having breakfast, this is also a moment to fully feel the flavor and breath of the city of flowers. Under the typical cold weather, each Da Lat breakfast dish seems to carry a love and unique quality that is hard to match anywhere else. Let breakfast become a memorable highlight in your journey to discover Da Lat.

1. Have breakfast in Da Lat with beef noodle soup in Doan Vien stone bowl

Address:29B Tran Phu, Ward 4

Opening hours:06:00 – 15:45

Price:65,000 – 120,000 VND

An ideal choice for breakfast in Da Lat is hot Hue beef noodle soup in stone bowl at Doan Vien restaurant. A rich bowl of vermicelli, tender fragrant meat and sweet broth will warm your heart in the cold weather.

2. Shumai bread - Famous breakfast dish in Da Lat at 37 Hoang Dieu

Address:26 & 37 Hoang Dieu, Ward 5

Opening hours:06:00 – 09:00

Price:15,000 VND

Shumai sandwichat 37 Hoang Dieu, Da Lat, has long been a familiar destination for those who are passionate about cuisine here. With an affordable price and rich flavor, this dish easily conquers diners right from the first taste. The crispy bread on the outside combines harmoniously with hot shumai and delicious slices of pork sausage, all creating an ideal breakfast in the cold weather of Da Lat.

3. An snakehead fish soup cake - Breakfast in Da Lat with Central taste

Address:23/2 Tran Phu, Ward 3

Opening hours:06:30 – 19:00

Price:15,000 – 30,000 VND

The highlight of An restaurant is the blend of airy space and agile, professional service style. Snakehead fish at the restaurant is carefully selected and processed directly, ensuring freshness in each bowl of noodle soup. That is what makes snakehead fish soup here an attractive choice for breakfast in Da Lat.

4. Experience hot pan bread when having breakfast in Da Lat at Icolor

Address:23/2 Dang Thai Than, Ward 3

Opening hours:07:00 – 11:00

Price:45,000 – 65,000 VND

Enjoying breakfast at Icolor restaurant in Da Lat, you will experience hot pan bread, combined with rich shumai sauce. Amid the chilly weather typical of the city of flowers, the dish brings a feeling of warmth and full flavor in every bite. With a variety of toppings such as pate, sausage, shumai and crispy bread, the breakfast here is not only delicious but also full of nutrition.

5. Nha Shin's Quang Noodles - The flavor of Quang in the heart of Da Lat for a complete breakfast

Address:209 Nguyen Cong Tru, Ward 2

Opening hours:06:00 – 14:00 (Tuesday–Sunday), 09:00 – 17:00 (Monday)

Price:25,000 – 40,000 VND

Nha Shin Quang Noodles is one of the unique breakfast dishes in Da Lat, imbued with the traditional flavors of Quang Nam cuisine. The restaurant was opened by a Quang-origin owner, thanks to which the flavor always remains standard and rich. Diners coming here can choose from many attractive variations such as Quang beef noodles, chicken, frog, snakehead fish or eel - all of which are carefully prepared, suitable for diverse tastes.

6. Have breakfast in Hoa Binh Da Lat area - Morning food paradise

6.1 Trang chicken wet cake

Address:15F Tang Bat Ho & 3B Mai Son Trang

Opening hours:06:00 – 18:00

Price:30,000 – 66,000 VND

It is not an exaggeration to say that chicken intestine wet cake has become one of the typical dishes of Da Lat cuisine. Visit Trang restaurant in the morning, you will enjoy a plate of soft, moist cakes, served with sweet and aromatic shredded chicken and attractive crispy chicken intestines. The chicken here is moderately chewy, not crumbly, skillfully seasoned, mixed with rich fish sauce, creating an unforgettable flavor for diners.

6.2 Pho Hieu – Breakfast option in Da Lat near the center

Address:103 Nguyen Van Troi

Opening hours:06:00 – 20:30

Price:20,000 – 80,000 VND

Located near the center of Da Lat, Pho Hieu restaurant is an ideal stop for breakfast, lunch or dinner. The menu is rich with options such as rare beef pho, tendon, fried, and mixed, each bowl has a rich, harmonious flavor. The broth is clear, sweet and not too fatty, creating a pleasant feeling when enjoying. An interesting point here is that you can eat it with quiche - a novel but extremely attractive combination.

6.3 Tung Cafe – Nostalgic space for a relaxing morning

Address:6 Hoa Binh area

Opening hours:07:00 – 21:30

Price:15,000 – 55,000 VND

Tung Cafe offers a unique culinary experience for breakfast in Da Lat. Not only famous for its unique coffee flavor, the shop also serves quality dishes, helping diners recharge for their exciting journey to explore the city of flowers.

If you're wondering what to eat for breakfast in Da Lat, noodles mixed with soft-boiled eggs are definitely a dish you should try. The noodle dish at the restaurant is attractive because of the harmonious combination of flavorful char siu, tender shredded chicken, creamy soft eggs, crispy pork rinds and fresh green vegetables. In particular, the broth is simmered from fresh chicken, without using MSG, bringing a natural sweetness, making this breakfast restaurant unique.

Hong chicken noodle soup is a unique breakfast dish in Da Lat, impressing with the special toughness of the noodles, almost like vermicelli, creating a new feeling when enjoying. The broth is made from stewed chicken, with a gentle aroma and characteristic rich taste. The chicken at the restaurant is soft, fragrant, and the skin is lightly crispy, creating a wonderful harmony when eaten with noodles. When combined with fresh basil and cilantro, the dish becomes even more fragrant and attractive.

14. Huong Quan - Breakfast address in Da Lat loved by locals

Address:47B Tran Binh Trong, Ward 5, Da Lat

Opening hours:9:00 - 22:00

Price:20,000 – 60,000 VND

If you want to find a breakfast restaurant in Da Lat that meets local standards, Huong Quan is an option not to be missed. This place stands out with its unique chicken dish, Central Highlands specialties and breakfast with vermicelli and pho rich in fragrant broth. The cozy space is the plus point that attracts diners.

15. Banh Mi 27 – Breakfast restaurant in Da Lat for Western Vietnamese food lovers

Address:105 Nguyen Luong Bang, Ward 2, Da Lat

Opening hours:6:30 - 14:00

Price:20,000 - 30,000 VND

Banh Mi 27 is an attractive breakfast restaurant in Da Lat, only about 2km from the market. Each portion of pan bread here is filled with pate, eggs, beef, sausage... hot on a cast iron pan, suitable for both adults and children.

16. Pho Uyen – A breakfast pho restaurant in Da Lat that has been loved for many years

Address:10 Xo Viet Nghe Tinh, Ward 7, Da Lat

Opening hours:7:00 – 16:30

Price:25,000 – 35,000 VND

A steaming bowl of pho, sweet broth from the bones and tender, fragrant beef - that's what you will enjoy at Pho Uyen. This is a breakfast place in Da Lat that has been with residents and tourists for many years.

17. Gia Cat noodle soup – A spacious and clean breakfast destination in Da Lat

Address:221/9 Hai Ba Trung, Ward 6, Da Lat

Opening hours:6:00 - 22:00

Price:50,000 - 60,000 VND

Gia Cat noodle soup is a breakfast restaurant in Da Lat with an airy and clean space. The Nam Vang noodle bowl here is filled with toppings such as meat, shrimp, quail eggs... with two options: dry or liquid according to taste.

18. Moist chicken intestine cake at 28 Tang Bat Ho – Famous breakfast dish in Da Lat

Address:28 Tang Bat Ho, Ward 1, Da Lat

Opening hours:6:00 – 20:00

Price:35,000 – 120,000 VND

When it comes to breakfast in Da Lat, chicken nuggets are an indispensable dish. Restaurant 28 Tang Bat Ho is a prominent name with thin, smooth cake, tender chicken, rich dipping sauce and many attractive side dishes such as salad, porridge, and young eggs.

19. Pho Chu Gia – Experience unique Da Lat breakfast pho in a stone bowl

Address:29 Thong Thien Hoc, Ward 2, Da Lat

Opening hours:6:00 - 22:00

Price:60,000 – 130,000 VND

Unlike other breakfast restaurants in Da Lat, Pho Chu Gia offers an interesting experience when eating pho in a boiling stone bowl. Rare beef and fresh pho noodles are served separately, enhancing their unique flavor and keeping them hot longer.

20. Thanh Thuy Coffee - Breakfast cafe in Da Lat with view of Xuan Huong lake

Address:2 Nguyen Thai Hoc, Da Lat

Opening hours:6:30 - 22:00

Price:50,000 – 132,000 VND

Thanh Thuy Coffee is an ideal breakfast place in Da Lat for those who love the romantic scenery beside Xuan Huong Lake. The restaurant serves breakfast dishes such as pho, rib rice, fried dumplings... along with signature coffee, blended in a cool green space.

6.4 Banh Can at 14 Tang Bat Ho – A Da Lat breakfast spot young people love

Address: 30 Tang Bat Ho

Opening hours: 13:00 – 22:00

Price: VND 15,000 – 20,000

The banh can shop at 14 Tang Bat Ho stands out for a varied menu that gives you plenty of choice at breakfast in Da Lat. From chicken egg and quail egg versions to the beef one, each has its own character. The real highlight is the dipping sauce – rich pork meatballs and a deep, savoury fish sauce – which brings out the flavour of every little cake.

7. Di Canh bun rieu – A humble but deeply flavoured Da Lat breakfast

Address: Alley 23/6 Ba Trieu, Ward 1

Opening hours: 06:00 – 10:00

Price: VND 25,000 – 35,000

Di Canh serves a tempting bun rieu loaded with toppings – spring rolls, crab roe, minced crab meat, pork blood and more. The steaming bowl is rich but well balanced, and the fresh raw herbs served alongside keep it light and bright.

8. Thien Trang bun bo Hue – A Da Lat breakfast with central Vietnamese flavour

Address: 2B Ho Tung Mau, Ward 10

Opening hours: 06:00 – 15:00

Price: VND 30,000 – 50,000

When exploring breakfast in Da Lat, Thien Trang's bun bo Hue is one you should not miss. The bowls are seasoned just right and generously filled with toppings, served with fresh herbs. The tables are set with satay chilli, chilli fish sauce, lime and fresh chilli so you can adjust the flavour to your own taste.

9. Dry noodles with soft-boiled egg – An unusual Da Lat breakfast

Address: 66 Nguyen Luong Bang, Ward 2

Opening hours: 07:00 – 19:30

Price: VND 30,000 – 40,000

If you cannot decide what to have for breakfast in Da Lat, these dry noodles with a soft-boiled egg are worth trying. The dish works because everything is in balance: savoury char siu, tender shredded chicken, a rich runny yolk, crisp pork crackling and fresh greens. The broth is simmered from fresh chicken with no MSG, giving a clean natural sweetness that sets this place apart.

10. Nhan Ngai banh mi – A long-standing Da Lat breakfast favourite

Address: 215 Bui Thi Xuan, Ward 8

Opening hours: 06:00 – 21:00

Price: VND 13,000 – 24,000

Nhan Ngai is one of the best-loved breakfast addresses in Da Lat, winning over visitors and locals alike with a flavour that is hard to forget. The bread is baked just right – crisp outside, still soft within. The filling is a careful mix of grilled pork, rich pâté, Vietnamese sausage and cold cuts, coming together into something rounded and savoury that is easy to fall for.

11. Banh uot Chong – An ideal Da Lat breakfast for a group of friends

Address: Alley 85 Hai Ba Trung, Ward 6

Opening hours: 08:00 – 21:00

Price: VND 20,000 – 30,000

These steamed rice rolls are a strong choice for breakfast in Da Lat – thin, springy sheets brought to the table while still hot. Every mouthful balances the delicate rolls against generous fillings. They come with Vietnamese sausage, bean sprouts, fried shallots, fresh herbs and fermented pork roll, plus the shop's four signature dipping sauces, which are what make the flavour so distinctive.

12. Pho 65 – The essence of Hanoi's Pho Thin, served for breakfast in Da Lat

Address: 291 Hai Ba Trung, Ward 6

Opening hours: 08:30 – 22:30

Price: VND 40,000 – 55,000

Among the breakfast places worth trying in Da Lat, Pho Dung 65 is one you cannot skip. It is known for Pho Thin – the famous Hanoi institution – with a deep, distinctive broth made to a family recipe. Knowing that tastes differ, the shop offers rare beef, brisket and bone-in cuts, or a pairing with a rich free-range soft-boiled egg, for a breakfast that feels complete.

13. Hong chicken hu tieu – A Da Lat breakfast you will struggle to find elsewhere

Address: At the end of Le Quy Don street

Opening hours: 10:00 – 17:00

Price: VND 20,000 – 30,000

Hong's chicken hu tieu is a distinctive Da Lat breakfast, memorable for noodles with an unusual chew, closer to glass noodles, which makes eating it feel novel. The broth is simmered from chicken, gently fragrant and full of flavour. The chicken is tender and aromatic, with lightly crisp skin that pairs beautifully with the noodles. Add fresh Thai basil and sawtooth coriander and the whole bowl lifts.

Photo: Hong chicken noodle soup - breakfast in Da Lat

14. Huong Quan – A Da Lat breakfast address the locals love

Address: 47B Tran Binh Trong, Ward 5, Da Lat

Serving hours: 9:00 – 22:00

Price: VND 20,000 – 60,000

If you want breakfast in Da Lat exactly as the locals have it, Huong Quan is not to be missed. It is known for its clay-jar chicken, Central Highlands specialities, and breakfast bowls of bun and pho with a deep, fragrant broth. The cosy space is what keeps people coming back.

15. Banh mi chao 27 – A Da Lat breakfast for lovers of Viet-Western food

Address: 105 Nguyen Luong Bang, Ward 2, Da Lat

Serving hours: 6:30 – 14:00

Price: VND 20,000 – 30,000

Banh mi chao 27 is an appealing breakfast spot in Da Lat, only about 2 km from the market. Each skillet comes loaded with pâté, egg, beef and sausage, sizzling on cast iron – good for adults and children alike.

16. Pho Uyen – A Da Lat breakfast pho loved for years

Address: 10 Xo Viet Nghe Tinh, Ward 7, Da Lat

Serving hours: 7:00 – 16:30

Price: VND 25,000 – 35,000

A steaming bowl, a clean sweet broth from the bones and tender, fragrant beef – that is what you get at Pho Uyen. It has been a fixture of breakfast in Da Lat for locals and visitors for many years.

17. Gia Cat hu tieu – A spacious, spotless Da Lat breakfast spot

Address: 221/9 Hai Ba Trung, Ward 6, Da Lat

Serving hours: 6:00 – 22:00

Price: VND 50,000 – 60,000

Gia Cat is a Da Lat breakfast place with an airy, spotless room. The Phnom Penh-style hu tieu comes piled with pork, prawns and quail eggs, served dry or in broth as you prefer.

18. Banh uot long ga at 28 Tang Bat Ho – A celebrated Da Lat breakfast

Address: 28 Tang Bat Ho, Ward 1, Da Lat

Serving hours: 6:00 – 20:00

Price: VND 35,000 – 120,000

No conversation about breakfast in Da Lat is complete without banh uot long ga. The shop at 28 Tang Bat Ho stands out for its silky thin rolls, tender chicken, a punchy dipping sauce and tempting sides such as salad, congee and immature eggs.

19. Chu Gia stone-pot pho – A distinctive Da Lat breakfast in a hot stone bowl

Address: 29 Thong Thien Hoc, Ward 2, Da Lat

Serving hours: 6:00 – 22:00

Price: VND 60,000 – 130,000

Unlike other breakfast places in Da Lat, Chu Gia serves its pho in a stone bowl that arrives bubbling. The rare beef and fresh rice noodles come separately, which sharpens the flavour and keeps everything hot for longer.

20. Thanh Thuy Coffee – A Da Lat breakfast café overlooking Xuan Huong Lake

Address: 2 Nguyen Thai Hoc, Da Lat

Serving hours: 6:30 – 22:00

Price: VND 50,000 – 132,000

Thanh Thuy Coffee is the ideal Da Lat breakfast for anyone who loves the dreamy scenery beside Xuan Huong Lake. It serves pho, rice with grilled pork chop, fried buns and its signature coffee, all in a cool, green setting.

Photo: Thanh Thuy Coffee - breakfast in Da Lat

21. Co Ba's xoi xeo – A humble Da Lat breakfast with northern flavour

Address: 5 Bach Dang, Ward 7, Da Lat

Serving hours: 08:00 – 20:30

Indicative price: VND 20,000 – 50,000

A familiar Da Lat morning sight is the queue outside Co Ba's for sticky rice. The menu is not long, but every dish is made with care and keeps its traditional flavour. The standouts are the nutty xoi xeo, the savoury version, and the northern-style sticky rice with braised pork. Co Ba herself is not only a fine cook but genuinely warm, which is a large part of why the place feels so welcoming.

22. Ba Huong's banh beo – Over 50 years keeping an old flavour alive

Address: 402 Phan Dinh Phung, Ward 2, Da Lat

Serving hours: 09:00 – 20:00

Indicative price: VND 30,000 – 44,000

Talk about banh beo in Da Lat and Ba Huong's name comes up – a place woven into the lives of several generations of locals. The little rice cakes are soft and smooth, fragrant with roasted peanuts and fried shallots, set against a rich, nutty topping. Each one carries more than half a century of the family's experience and care.

23. Hoa Tran chicken glass noodles – Deep flavour for a chilly morning

Address: 126 Thang 2 Street, Ward 1, Da Lat

Serving hours: 09:00 – 21:00

Indicative price: VND 35,000 – 150,000

A bowl at Hoa Tran brings together springy glass noodles, golden poached free-range chicken and a clean, sweet broth. Add a few herbs, a slice of lime and some chilli, and it does more than warm you up – the flavour is deep and finely judged.

24. Tui Mo To Coffee – A lovely way to start the day

Address: Alley 31 Sao Nam, Ward 11, Da Lat

Serving hours: 07:45 – 21:30

Indicative price: VND 30,000 – 65,000

Tui Mo To is not just a good-looking Da Lat café; it also does a light breakfast of eggs and bread, sandwiches and pastries. The rustic room and the soft early light let you take in Da Lat's clean air properly.

25. Vinh Loi noodles – A comforting bowl of char siu noodles each morning

Address: 3 Le Quy Don, Ward 5, Da Lat

Serving hours:

  • Mon, Wed – Sun: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • Tue: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Indicative price: VND 40,000 – 50,000

Vinh Loi is known for a clear broth, clean and sweet from long-simmered bones, with springy noodles and fragrant char siu. Simple but finely judged, it is the ideal way to start a long day exploring the mountain town.

26. Cafe Moc – A taste of home in the heart of Da Lat

Address: Alley 8, Phu Thuan Street, Tan Phu Ward, Da Lat

Serving hours: 08:00 – 19:00

Indicative price: VND 15,000 – 35,000

Cafe Moc is a Da Lat breakfast spot with an old-fashioned feel. Beyond coffee it serves hu tieu, banh nam, mi quang and noodle soups. Everything is made with care, and eating here feels like a warm family meal.

27. Pho Chat – Good pho in a rustic setting

Address: 17/5 Khoi Nghia Bac Son, Ward 10, Da Lat

Serving hours: 07:30 – 19:00

Indicative price: VND 35,000 – 45,000

Beyond the traditional flavours – a clean sweet broth, soft noodles and fresh beef – the place scores for a relaxed room close to nature. It even offers free shower facilities for travellers.

28. Singapore frog congee – Something unusual in the mountain town

Address: 151 Bui Thi Xuan, Ward 2, Da Lat

Serving hours: 10:30 – 22:00

Indicative price: VND 20,000 – 77,000

Singapore frog congee at 151 is a favourite among food lovers. The firm frog meat is braised in a clay pot with lemongrass, chilli and ginger, then set against soft, fragrant plain congee. It tastes good and keeps you warm in the cool air.

29. Chao Long 5A – A well-known congee shop in Da Lat

Address: 140 Phu Dong Thien Vuong, Ward 8, Da Lat

Serving hours: 06:30 – 19:00

Indicative price: VND 30,000 – 40,000

A roomy, clean space, generous bowls and carefully prepared offal are why 5A is always busy. The congee is rich and piping hot – an excellent choice on a cool Da Lat morning.

30. Tau Cao wonton noodles – A long-established place with Chinese character

Address: 217 Phan Dinh Phung, Da Lat

Serving hours: 06:00 – 21:00

Indicative price: VND 35,000 – 40,000

Tau Cao is a Da Lat breakfast address known for its wonton noodles. Springy noodles, large well-filled wontons and a clean sweet broth keep diners coming back. The cooking carries a distinctly Chinese character, and the shop is a familiar stop for locals.
