1. Tuyen Lam Lake – A chill spot in Da Lat close to nature

No list would be complete without chill spot in Da Lat, Tuyen Lam Lake stands out with its still water surrounded by endless pine forest, creating a scene that is both dreamy and peaceful. It is the ideal place to rest, breathe fresh air, kayak at sunset, or simply sit quietly in nature and let your mind heal.

Suggested activities:

  • Camping and picnics

  • Check-in Da Lat with shimmering photos of the lake at dawn

Thien Phuc Duc Hill là places to go in Da Lat is ideal for anyone who loves stillness and untouched nature. Here you can hunt clouds at dawn, watch a magical sunrise, and above all take photos with the Lonely Tree – the famous photo icon tied to Da Lat's dreamy image.

3. Cu Lan Village – A chill spot in Da Lat for families

If you are looking for a chill spot in Da Lat that combines green space with cultural experiences, then Cu Lan Village is a genuinely interesting choice. Set in wild natural scenery, the village also offers activities such as rafting, visiting stilt houses and learning about ethnic minority life – perfect for families and groups of friends who want to play, relax and bond.

Suggested activities:

  • Visit the K'ho ethnic village

  • Rafting and horse riding

  • Check-in Da Lat with the wooden bridge and traditional houses

📍 Address: Lat Commune, Lac Duong District, Lam Dong Province

4. Doi May Tourist Area – A chill spot in Da Lat with cloud-hunting views

Doi May (Cloud Hill) is a much-loved chill spot in Da Lat thanks to its perfect mix of café, cloud hunting and scenery. Sitting high on a hill, it has a superb view over a drifting sea of clouds at dawn, plus open space that is ideal for relaxing, taking check-in photos or sipping a warm coffee in the crisp air.

Suggested activities:

  • Enjoy coffee among the clouds

  • Photo ops with the greenhouse and rattan chairs

  • Create photos that are check-in Da Lat one of a kind

📍 Address: Ward 11, TP. Da Lat

If you would rather not travel far, Xuan Huong Lake is exactly the places to go in Da Lat in the heart of the city, ideal for strolling, cycling around the lake, having coffee on the shore and feeling the calm that defines Da Lat.   • Pedal boats and lakeside walks

  • Watch a romantic sunset and check-in Da Lat at night

You cannot skip Da Lat Market – home to hundreds of tempting street snacks and local specialities. It is not only a food paradise but also a spot check-in Da Lat that comes alive at night with bright lights, a buzzing atmosphere and photo corners you simply cannot resist.

If you want to chill and enjoy delicious grilled food in a distinctive setting, Xom Leo Grill & Chill is the ideal destination. The place is simple in style yet genuinely cosy, letting you make the most of Da Lat's cool weather.
